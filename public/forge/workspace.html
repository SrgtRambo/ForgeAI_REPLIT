Settings

AI generation and your project data.

Forge AI

When enabled, Forge AI asks discovery follow-up questions, drafts the architecture, generates application source files, and proposes fixes for failed checks. When disabled, Forge uses its clearly labeled offline starter templates. AI prompts are sent to OpenRouter; do not include passwords, API keys, or other secrets. Requests are limited to 20 per minute and 300 per day.

NOT CHECKED
Forge AI builds the page, styles, and behavior in separate resumable steps. Review every generated app in the preview before relying on it.

Project data

Your discovery answers, architectures, generated source, and version history are saved privately to your account. Only you can see them. Apps you generate keep their own records in the browser where they run.

You can export all your projects as JSON or permanently delete them from your account.
